Glossary & Terms

TMC
Total Manufacturing Cost. The sum of Direct Materials, Direct Labor, and Overhead used.
DM / DL / MOH
Direct Materials, Direct Labor, and Manufacturing OverHead.
COGM
Cost of Goods Manufactured. The total cost of products that finished production during the period.
COGS
Cost of Goods Sold. The accumulated total of all costs used to create a product or service, which has been sold.
WIP / FG
Work In Process (partially completed goods) and Finished Goods (fully completed goods).
TEU
Total Equivalent Units. A measure of production used in process costing to account for partially finished units.
CPEU
Cost Per Equivalent Unit. Total costs incurred divided by total equivalent units.
Est Driver Amount
The estimated base used to allocate overhead (e.g., machine hours, direct labor hours).
