Managerial Accounting

CVP Analysis Tools

1. Break-Even Point (Units)

BE PT Units =
Fixed Costs CM per Unit
Result (Units): --

Variables:

  • BE PT Units: The exact number of units you need to sell to make $0 profit (covering all costs).
  • Fixed Costs: Expenses that do not change regardless of how many units you sell (e.g., rent, salaries).
  • CM per Unit: Contribution Margin per unit. Calculated as (Sales Price per unit - Variable Cost per unit).

2. Break-Even Point (Sales $$)

BE PT Sales $$ =
Fixed Costs CM %
Result (Sales Revenue): --

Variables:

  • BE PT Sales $$: The total dollar amount of revenue needed to break even.
  • Fixed Costs: Total unchanging expenses for the period.
  • CM %: Contribution Margin Ratio. The percentage of each sales dollar available to cover fixed costs.

3. Target Profit Point in Units

Target Profit
Point in Units =
Fixed Costs + TP CM per Unit
Result (Units): --

Variables:

  • Target Profit (TP): The specific amount of net income the company wishes to earn.
  • Target Profit Point: How many units must be sold to cover costs AND achieve the target profit.

4. Contribution Margin Ratio (CM %)

CM % =
CM Sales
Result (Percentage): --

Variables:

  • CM: Total Contribution Margin (Total Sales minus Total Variable Costs).
  • Sales: Total Sales Revenue.
  • CM %: Translates the contribution margin into a percentage format, showing how much of every sales dollar is left after variable costs.

5. Degree of Operating Leverage

Operating
Leverage =
Contribution Margin Net Income
Result (Multiplier): --

Variables:

  • Contribution Margin: Total Sales minus Total Variable Expenses.
  • Net Income: The total profit of the company after all expenses (Variable and Fixed) are deducted from sales.
  • Operating Leverage: A multiplier. If operating leverage is 4, a 10% increase in sales will result in a 40% (10% x 4) increase in net income.
